# Surface: mockups/index.html — four phone-app mockup sets (Cast, Familiars, Detour, Palate)

Scope: one published page holding, per app, three to four phone screens plus the big-screen (projector) view, with real copy, real states, and labeled demonstration data. Visitor mode of the page: Experience (the screens lead; the page recedes). Visitor mode of each screen: Operate. Audience: the team tonight, then mentors and judges at 09:00. Job: align on look and feel per app before building; compete for Best Design. Constraint: mobile-web PWA screens, no native iOS chrome; four independent identities; not AI-slop; names final.

## Direction contract — Cast

THESIS: The cast is a strip of paper everyone writes on hidden and unfolds together. It refuses the BeReal feed: no cards, no timeline of posts, one strip a day, a pond of strips beneath.
OWN-WORLD: Near-black table ground (#0E0F12); a bone paper strip (#F7F4EC) creased into one panel per member with real 3D folds and crease shadows; felt-tip ink, one color per member (orange #FF4A1F, blue #2B6BFF, yellow #FFD028, green #1FB36B); the cast prompt in Anybody (wide, black weight) at enormous size; member ink in Shantell Sans; tiny grotesk labels; no rounded cards, no rules except folds.
STORY: The phone buzzes with everyone else's; you write on your panel; it folds shut and floods with your color; when the last panel folds the strip unfolds top to bottom and the circle reads together; in catch mode the plan is a strip with a line to pull, live only at quorum. The Pond is the strips stacked by day at one scale.
FIRST VIEWPORT: Screen 1 (the cast): countdown strip tearing shorter across the top, the cast in 4-line huge type, the paper strip below with your open panel and three folded colored panels; primary action = the fold button on the paper's edge. Screens 2–4: reveal (unfolded strip), catch (the line, tension, 3 of 4), the Pond. Big screen: the Pond with K2's reasoning as a fishing line down the side.
FORM: Fold-and-pass paper game; candidate 5 of 7 on my ordered list; seed key 3b934a9d, assigned; raises from racing (color flood), folio (fixed scale), dive (one vertical axis), specimen (scale-only hierarchy).

STORY: Insert coin = three answers; your familiar hatches; bump two phones and the two sprites walk to the middle of the screen, a speech box shows the ten-second exchange, CONTACT +1 flashes in power gold; the village on the projector is a tile map of every familiar, edges as pixel paths; at the end your night's story scrolls as a text box, ending with the one that got away.
FIRST VIEWPORT: Screen 1: score line (CONTACTS 07 · BUMPS 09), your familiar sprite large at center on a tile floor, the three seed answers as a pixel text box, the primary action BUMP as a pixel-bordered arcade-blue button. Screens 2–4: the bump exchange, the night's story, the one that got away. Big screen: the village tile map.
FORM: CRT arcade pixel glow (catalog medium-native-crt-arcade-pixel-glow); chosen challenger over the assigned QSL card; seed key b3e8a1ae; kept from the assigned: every familiar has a three-character address as the bump fallback.

## Direction contract — Detour

THESIS: You are shown one map fragment and one red arrow, never the map. It refuses the map app: no blue dot, no route line, no bottom sheet, no north.
OWN-WORLD: White void (#FFFFFF); scissor-cut black-and-white map fragments (authored SVG street snippets of Oakland with real street names in Archivo Narrow italic caps) drifting at a slight rotation; one fat curved red arrow (#E4322B) authored as SVG; the nudge sentence in Archivo Black at large size, one line only; time remaining as a thin red bar at the top edge; grays (#8C8C8C) for the walked fragments; nothing else. Big screen: the cut-up assembling, red line joining fragments, the solver's grid faint underneath.
STORY: Set 45 minutes, an endpoint, a mood; the phone shows a fragment and an arrow and one sentence; you look up; the next fragment replaces it in a step; the walked city dissolves behind you; the wall: the destination is never shown; you arrive on time.

## Direction contract — Palate

OWN-WORLD: Deep chart blue (#0A1230) sky disc with fine constellation lines (#3A4C8A); a bone horizon mask (#F5F1E6) with an oval window and a brass rim (#C8A24A) carrying menu sections as hour numerals in Saira Condensed; dish-stars in warm white (#FFF6DC) and gold (#FFD36A), families joined by lines; below-horizon dishes dim blue (#4B5FA6); unknown dishes grey unmarked points (#8C8C8C); labels in Saira; all text achromatic; selection = a thin ring that keeps turning.
STORY: Log five dishes you love and the sky fills; point at a menu and the rim takes its sections; drag to rotate; the window shows what's for you tonight; table mode overlays windows and the shared stars glow; the wall: grey points stay grey until the kitchen answers.

FORM: Planisphere star chart; candidate 5 of 7; seed key 393dfb9c, assigned; raises from cloud-edge (color confined to stars and rim), one-bit (hard selection state), drum (one unbroken ring that scales).
