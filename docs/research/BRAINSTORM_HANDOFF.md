# HackCMU brainstorming handoff

Prepared for a fresh ideation/evaluation run. This is a catalog, not a commitment to a project or a fresh ranking.

## Context to preserve

- The user values novel ideas and interesting experiences over technical difficulty for its own sake. A compelling map can be the product; it need not support a dramatic personal number.
- The user explicitly found **Cinderella, The Long Trade/Knot, Quorum, Fault Line, and Five Meals** interesting, but not quite there. They rejected the crispness-meter direction as uninteresting and technically awkward.
- Main tracks recorded in the repo: Traveling, Optimization, Multiplayer, Food. **IFM is an add-on; the user has settled this.** Do not reopen that question.
- Earlier workflows assumed four people, two with unknown skills, limited overnight build time, and a three-minute judging-room demo. Verify current team/time constraints before making a new implementation plan.
- Do not inherit guesses about track crowding, judges' preferences, sponsor eligibility, or prize odds as facts. “Exactly one algorithm,” “must reveal something about the judge,” and “must surprise someone at dinner” were an agent-authored rubric, not official requirements.
- No build choice has been authorized by this catalog. The repo currently has scaffolding and an older Last Train plan; prototypes are in Claude's temporary scratchpad.

## How to read the table

The extra column is **Evidence / next check**: it distinguishes a promising sentence from something already tested and identifies the cheapest decisive follow-up. “Prototype” means exploratory code, not a validated product. “Historical review” means a conclusion in Claude's logs that has not necessarily been independently rechecked. Why/why not are current synthesis judgments, not scores.

This catalog has **103 consolidated entries covering 106 structured source proposals and two additional synthesis branches** (Cinderella and medical Right of Passage). Five redundant entries are merged: KNOT/The Long Trade; Snackoscope/Decibite under Crispness Meter; OVERLAP/THIRD PLACE/round-one LAST CALL; and ABILENE/ONE PERSON AWAY. Same-name concepts with different mechanisms remain separate. Crunchprint remains separate because snack identification differs from measuring crispness. The companion TSV adds stable B001–B103 identifiers for batching.
